While Sanya Phoenix International Airport is the primary choice, there are alternative airports that may suit your travel plans. (1) Haikou Meilan International Airport (HAK), Located approximately 3.5 hours from Sanya by high-speed rail or car, Haikou serves as another major international gateway to Hainan Island. (2) Hong Kong International Airport (HKG), For some travelers, flying into Hong Kong and then connecting to Sanya via a short direct flight (about 1.5 hours) may be a convenient option. Whether you're flying directly into Sanya or connecting through another major hub, getting to the host city is straightforward and well-supported by multiple travel options.
