The Sanya Phoenix International Airport (SYX) is your gateway to SAUVC Sanya. Conveniently located about 15 kilometers from downtown Sanya, the airport offers excellent connectivity with domestic and international destinations. You'll find a variety of ground transportation options including taxis, ride-hailing services, airport shuttles, and hotel transfers to reach your accommodations and the conference venue with ease.
