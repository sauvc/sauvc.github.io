By Ride-Hailing or Hotel Shuttle-  Popular apps such as Didi Chuxing (China's version of Uber) operate in Sanya and provide direct service from the airport to the venue. By bus- Sanya has over sixty bus routes, operating from around 06:00 to 23:00. The ticket prices follow a distance-based fare system. In addition, there are many privately-operated shuttle buses.
